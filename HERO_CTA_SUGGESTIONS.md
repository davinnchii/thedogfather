# Hero CTA Text Suggestions

Current: **"Send forespørsel"**

## Action-Oriented CTAs

1. **"Kom i kontakt"** - Simple and friendly
2. **"Book time"** - Direct and clear
3. **"Start din reise"** - Inspiring and forward-looking
4. **"Få hjelp nå"** - Urgent and helpful
5. **"Ta kontakt"** - Casual and approachable

## Service-Focused CTAs

6. **"Bestill konsultasjon"** - Professional
7. **"Få en uforpliktende samtale"** - Low-pressure
8. **"Kom i gang"** - Energetic and motivating
9. **"Utforsk tjenester"** - Discovery-focused
10. **"Se hvordan vi kan hjelpe"** - Solution-oriented

## Personal/Emotional CTAs

11. **"La oss møtes"** - Personal and warm
12. **"Bli en del av familien"** - Community-focused
13. **"Start i dag"** - Immediate action
14. **"Finn løsningen"** - Problem-solving focus
15. **"Opplev forskjellen"** - Experience-focused

## Short & Punchy

16. **"Kontakt meg"** - Direct and personal
17. **"Kom i gang"** - Simple and action-oriented
18. **"Start nå"** - Immediate and clear
19. **"Finn ut mer"** - Curiosity-driven
20. **"Book time"** - Clear call-to-action

## Recommendations

**Top 3 picks:**
1. **"Kom i kontakt"** - Friendly, approachable, works for all contexts
2. **"Book time"** - Clear, direct, action-oriented
3. **"Ta kontakt"** - Casual, Norwegian-friendly, low barrier

**For a dog training service, I'd recommend:**
- **"Kom i kontakt"** - Most versatile and friendly
- **"Book time"** - If you want to emphasize scheduling
- **"Få en uforpliktende samtale"** - If you want to reduce friction
